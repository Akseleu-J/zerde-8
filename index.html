Invented words in The Lord of the Rings and Avatar

We compared 40 invented words from Middle-earth and Pandora. We wanted to see how they are formed and what they tell us about each world. This site holds the quiz, the dictionary and the diary we made from that work.

What we found

How the two worlds build their words

In both lists, constructed languages were our largest source. In Middle-earth we found more older Germanic words and more compounds. On Pandora we found more short Na’vi words, more modern English and more words taken from real cultures.

The percentages we calculated for each list of 20 words.

Lord of the RingsAvatar

Hypothesis 1 — we found support

We expected more compound words in The Lord of the Rings, and the count agreed with us: 55% against 25% in Avatar. We also found more short Na’vi words in Avatar: 40% against 5%.

Hypothesis 2 — only partly

We expected more place names in Middle-earth, and that part held: 30% against 10%. Pandora gave us more words for society and spiritual life. Creatures were 30% in both lists, so we can support this hypothesis only in part.
